Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview
StackScope is an independent job market intelligence tool. It scrapes public job board listings, processes them with NLP, and surfaces skill demand data. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate and do not share or sell anything.
Data we collect
StackScope does not collect personal information from visitors. No account registration is required. No cookies are used for tracking. The skill analyser runs entirely in your browser and no skill selections are transmitted or stored anywhere.
Job data
Job postings are scraped from public APIs and RSS feeds and stored in our database for analysis. We do not scrape or store any personal information about job applicants or hiring managers — only the job listing content itself (title, skills, salary range, location).
Analytics
This site may use Vercel Analytics to collect anonymised page view and performance data. No cookies are used and no personal identifiers are collected. Vercel's privacy policy governs this data.
Third-party services
  • Adzuna, Remotive, We Work Remotely, Jobspresso — public job data sources
  • Vercel — hosting and edge delivery
  • Render — backend hosting

External links to job boards are provided for convenience and are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies. StackScope is not affiliated with any of them.
